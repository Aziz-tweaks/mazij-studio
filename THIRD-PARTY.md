and keeps its own terms.

  Vazirmatn, each distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
  (https://openfontlicense.org/), sourced unmodified from Google Fonts.

Reference material owned by third parties is deliberately excluded from this
repository. See `app/.gitignore` and the README of each target under
`app/qa/replication/targets/`.

Full license texts are in [`LICENSES/`](LICENSES). Per-dependency attribution,
with copyright lines and where each is used, is in [`app/NOTICE`](app/NOTICE).
Non-code assets under `public/` are documented in
[`app/public/ASSETS.md`](app/public/ASSETS.md).
